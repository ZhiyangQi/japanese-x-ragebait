# Data use and responsible research

This repository distributes a minimized, ID-only index of the X Posts used in the accompanying study. It does not redistribute post text or the authors' per-Post research annotations.

## What is distributed

The release candidate contains two single-column files: `data/train_ids.csv` and `data/test_ids.csv`. They contain 16,558 and 2,000 X Post IDs, respectively.

## What is not distributed

The files contain no ragebait/non-ragebait label mapping, post text, usernames, profiles, direct URLs, media, annotation reasons, hydrated Post objects, or user-level attributes.

Inclusion means only that the Post was part of the study dataset. It does not mean that the Post was labeled as ragebait, and it must not be represented as such.

## Intended purpose

The resource is intended for non-commercial academic research, peer review, and validation of the accompanying WI-IAT 2026 study. Public availability remains subject to the required institutional and platform-policy checks.

## User responsibilities

Researchers are expected to:

1. Obtain X content only through an official, currently permitted interface using their own credentials.
2. Follow the current X Terms of Service, Developer Agreement, Developer Policy, and applicable law.
3. Respect deletion, protection, suspension, withholding, and other availability changes. Do not attempt to recover unavailable content.
4. Keep rehydrated content out of public repositories and downloadable datasets.
5. Avoid user profiling, identification, harassment, targeting, surveillance, or consequential decisions about individuals.
6. Apply appropriate safeguards when working with offensive, hateful, or otherwise sensitive content.

## Rights

No license to X content is granted by this repository. The Post IDs and any rehydrated content remain subject to X's then-current terms and the rights of the original authors. The repository's MIT License applies only to the authors' software and documentation, not to X content or the Post IDs.

## Corrections and removals

The maintainers will provide a public issue or contact channel for correction and identifier-removal concerns when the repository is published. A release may remove an identifier without replacing it when continued distribution is inappropriate.
